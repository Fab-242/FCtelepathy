How the trick works

Two accomplices are dealt 5 cards from a 52-card deck. The encoder keeps one of them (the hidden card) and hands the other 4 to the decoder, in a specific order, who must guess the hidden card.

  1. Same-suit pair. With 5 cards and 4 suits, at least two cards always share the same suit (pigeonhole principle). One of them becomes the signal card (the 1st card shown), the other stays hidden.
  2. Circular distance (1 to 6). On the circle A→2→3→…→K→A, there is always a direction in which the distance between the two same-suit cards is between 1 and 6. The starting card is the signal, the other end is the hidden card, and the number of steps is the delta.
  3. Encoding the delta (1-6) with 3 cards. The 3 remaining cards are sorted: Low < Middle < High. Ties in rank are broken using the suit order CHaSeD: ♣ Clubs < ♥ Hearts < ♠ Spades < ♦ Diamonds. There are 3! = 6 ways to order 3 cards, one per delta value:
DeltaOrder sent
1Low, Middle, High
2Low, High, Middle
3Middle, Low, High
4Middle, High, Low
5High, Low, Middle
6High, Middle, Low

Decoding: the 1st card received gives the hidden suit and the starting point. The order of the next 3 cards gives the delta. Hidden card = signal + delta (on the circle A→…→K→A).
